Local council approved a temporary pedestrian zone on Mill Street on Tuesday after a two-month pilot showed foot traffic rose by thirty-eight percent. The pilot closed one block to vehicles between ten in the morning and eight in the evening, leaving room for outdoor seating and small street performances. Shop owners in the area reported higher weekend sales, although some residents said noise levels late at night became a problem. Councillor Anna Reeve, who proposed the trial, said the council would collect more feedback before deciding whether to make the change permanent next spring. She also noted that deliveries to nearby businesses would still be allowed in the early morning. The decision followed several public meetings where neighbours, drivers and shopkeepers gave very different views. The mayor's office said it would publish a full report on the pilot within four weeks.
